Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Keetjet ("the game") is published by Smart360 ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

Who the game is for

Keetjet is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Information we collect

  • Game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • Advertising ID and ad interaction data are collected by Google AdMob to show and measure ads. In regions that require it, you are asked for consent first (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice any time in Settings > About > Manage ad consent.
  • Analytics and crash reports (device model, Android version, app version, anonymous usage events and crash logs) are collected by Google Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics to fix bugs and improve the game. They are not used to identify you.
  • Purchases (remove ads, coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details.
  • Play Games (optional) sign-in lets leaderboards and achievements work; it uses your Google Play Games profile.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and do not access your contacts, photos or microphone.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules a local reminder every evening at 9:00 pm. It is created on your device (not sent from a server). You can turn it off in Settings > Game > Daily reminder.

Data deletion

You can erase all game data stored on your device with Settings > About > Delete my data, or by uninstalling the game. To request deletion of analytics data linked to your device, contact us at the address below.

Third-party services

Google AdMob, Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Their policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
