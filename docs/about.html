About Keetjet

Keetjet is an arcade shooter for teens and adults. Fly a fighter jet through kitchens, rooftops, gardens, railway stations, the sea and even the Moon, and defend your home from swarms of bugs, animals and machines. Collect coins, unlock new jets, weapons and blasts, and defeat a unique boss in every stage.
